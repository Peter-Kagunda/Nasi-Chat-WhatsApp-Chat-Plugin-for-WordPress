## Turn curious visitors into real conversations

### Show who’s online before anyone clicks
Nothing kills a good first impression like messaging someone who’s asleep. With per-agent availability, visitors see exactly who can reply right now.
* A green dot for online, grey for offline, plus a friendly “Back Mon 8:00 am” for when someone returns.
* Custom weekly hours and a timezone for every team member.
* Follow your business hours, or keep an agent always online.
* The floating button turns grey on its own when nobody’s around.
* Status is worked out in the visitor’s browser, so it stays accurate even on sites with full-page caching.

### Make it look like it was built for your brand
Your chat box should feel like a natural part of your site, not a bolted-on widget. Tweak the colours, corners, width and photo shapes, and watch the live preview update as you go.
* Colours for the header, text, panel, hover, buttons, and online and offline status.
* Adjustable corner radius and chat panel width.
* Circle, rounded or square team photos.
* Your own panel title and welcome message.

### Speak every visitor’s language
Greet people in the language they browse in. Agent names, job titles, prefilled messages, tooltips, welcome and offline messages, status labels and form text are all ready to translate.
* It works with WPML and Polylang, and you can show each agent only on the languages they speak.

### Capture the lead before the chat begins
When a conversation jumps to WhatsApp, you shouldn’t lose track of who started it. Ask for a few details first, and every submission lands safely in your dashboard.
* Name, email address, phone number, topic and message.
* An optional GDPR consent checkbox.
* Built-in spam protection with a honeypot and rate limiting.

### Meet customers on their favourite channel
Not everyone lives in WhatsApp. Add a neat round button for Telegram, Messenger, SMS and Email beside it, and let each visitor choose what suits them.

### Every enquiry organised and ready for follow-up
Manage everything you collect without ever leaving WordPress.
* Browse captured leads, page by page.
* Delete records you no longer need.
* Export everything to CSV.
* Get an email the moment a new lead arrives.
* Send leads to Zapier, Make or your CRM with signed HMAC-SHA256 webhooks.

### Drop a chat button anywhere
Place the full chat box or a single button on any page, post, widget or page-builder block. Choose the agents, alignment and label. It works with or without the floating button.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Nasi Chat Pro?**  
Nasi Chat Pro is a premium add-on for the free Nasi Chat WordPress plugin. It adds team availability, chat customisation, lead capture, extra communication channels and shortcodes.

**Do I need the free Nasi Chat plugin?**  
Yes. Nasi Chat Pro needs the free Nasi Chat plugin installed and active. You can try it free [here](https://wordpress.org/plugins/nasi-chat-click-to-connect/).

**Do I need a WhatsApp Business account?**  
No. Nasi Chat works with any WhatsApp-enabled phone number.

**Can I show several team members?**  
Yes. Add your team in the free Nasi Chat plugin (names, job titles, photos and numbers), then set each person’s availability in Nasi Chat Pro.

**Does it work on multilingual websites?**  
Yes. Nasi Chat Pro supports WPML and Polylang, and every visitor-facing text can be translated.

**Does it work with page caching?**  
Yes. Online and offline status is calculated in the visitor’s browser, so it stays correct on sites that use full-page caching.

**Can I capture leads before a visitor starts chatting?**  
Yes. Turn on the pre-chat form and choose from name, email, phone number, topic, message and GDPR consent.

**Can I use one license on several websites?**  
Each license key works on one website at a time. If you move your site, deactivate the license first, then activate the same key on the new site.

**Where can I find setup instructions?**  
In the [Nasi Chat Pro documentation](https://peterkagunda.com/chat-nasi-pro-docs/).

**Is Nasi Chat Pro available on WordPress.org?**  
No. Nasi Chat Pro is sold separately and is not distributed through WordPress.org. The free Nasi Chat plugin is, and you can try it [here](https://wordpress.org/plugins/nasi-chat-click-to-connect/).
